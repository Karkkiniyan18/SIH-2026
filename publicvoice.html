The challenge

Policy consultation is rich in data — but poor in processing time.

Large-scale public feedback contains valuable signals, yet conventional review makes it difficult to surface them consistently.

⏱

Manual review overload

Teams spend weeks reading, sorting and summarising thousands of responses before meaningful patterns emerge.

⧉

Duplicate voices

Repeated or near-identical submissions inflate volume and make it harder to distinguish unique citizen concerns.

⌁

Lost insights

Important minority opinions, emerging themes and cross-topic connections can disappear inside unstructured feedback.
